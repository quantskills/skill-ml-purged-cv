# Preserve complete Information Intervals before applying Embargo

The trainable temporal benchmark keeps every sample's Information Interval from its earliest lag dependency through label completion, even when interval-aware Purge then makes a declared Embargo produce zero incremental exclusions. Narrowing intervals to manufacture a visible Embargo count would weaken leakage evidence; the report therefore discloses Embargo as active or redundant-after-Purge instead of changing temporal truth.
