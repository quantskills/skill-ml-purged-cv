# Governed full-cache CPCV effectiveness evaluation

Status: ready-for-agent
Owner: project owner
Risk: high, bounded local evidence

## Acceptance stories

1. Continuous assets are selected explicitly from vendor DOMINANT rows.
2. Concrete contracts cannot be silently merged into one commodity session.
3. Roll transitions, eligible rows, exclusions, and identity policy are receipted.
4. MSE, cross-sectional IC, and diagnostic Sharpe have frozen reproducible definitions.
5. CPCV reports every path plus distribution, worst path, and dispersion.
6. Purged K-Fold, CPCV, and Walk-Forward use the same data/model and expose coverage.
7. Native, common-period, and roll-clean sensitivity views are distinct.
8. Every fold reports training observations, sessions, assets, and sufficiency.
9. The full local cache produces redacted deterministic evidence within the bounded probe.

## Non-goals

Profitability, transaction costs, executable sizing, live rolling, vendor adjustment
verification, Untouched Holdout, deployment, and GitHub publication.
